Dental Cleaning

Professional hygiene and prophylaxis – fresh breath, healthy gums.

Our thorough dental cleaning removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains that daily brushing cannot reach. Using ultrasonic scalers and gentle hand instruments, our hygienists meticulously clean every tooth surface. Regular cleanings every 6 months help prevent cavities, gum disease, and bad breath. The session includes a full periodontal assessment, oral cancer screening, and personalised home‑care advice.
  • Removes stubborn plaque and tartar
  • Prevents gum disease (gingivitis/periodontitis)
  • Fresher breath and brighter smile
  • Early detection of dental issues
